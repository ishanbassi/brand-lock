# Free trademark portfolio management

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Make the free plan tangible for trademark attorneys and boutique IP practices: a live-looking practice portfolio proves the operational value before feature and pricing detail. This refuses the category-default page that opens with an abstract claim or a generic pricing table.

**OWN-WORLD:** Keep this public route in the supplied prototype's cool-lavender system: pale lavender surface, white cards, deep indigo calls to action, mint confirmation chips, and restrained red warnings. Use Plus Jakarta Sans throughout; surfaces have 8px or 12px rounding, soft low shadows, Material Symbols, and an airy maximum-width content grid. These route-level decisions do not redefine the authenticated portal's visual system.

**STORY:** A visitor immediately sees who the product serves, that portfolios of up to 100 trademarks are free, and the concrete work it supports. The page then validates the offer with plan limits and practice tools before repeating the account-setup action.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Below the shared fixed Agent Portal navbar, a two-column hero on a 12-column grid pairs the free-portfolio headline and indigo setup action on the left with a white Practice Portfolio preview card on the right. The preview contains three small metrics and three status rows; a mint verified pill and concise no-card/no-fees assurances support the action without competing with it.
