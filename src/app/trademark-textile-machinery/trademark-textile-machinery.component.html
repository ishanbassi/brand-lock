Why Textile & Hosiery Machinery Manufacturers in Punjab Need Trademark Protection

Ludhiana is one of India's largest hubs for textile and hosiery machinery, manufacturing circular knitting machines, flat knitting machines, sock knitting machines, warping and winding machines, and a vast range of machinery spare parts that are supplied across India and exported to Bangladesh, African and Middle Eastern garment industries.

With hundreds of ancillary units producing look-alike machines and interchangeable spare parts through overlapping dealer and export networks, copied brand names cast onto machine bodies, duplicated nameplates, and imitation catalogues reaching the same buyers are well-known hazards in this industry. A registered trademark gives you the exclusive legal right to your brand name and logo across India — enabling you to record your mark with Indian Customs, and act against counterfeiters through cease-and-desist notices, civil court injunctions, and takedowns on B2B export platforms.
