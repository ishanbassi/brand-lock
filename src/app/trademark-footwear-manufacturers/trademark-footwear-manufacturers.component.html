Why Footwear Manufacturers in Agra Need Trademark Protection

Agra's footwear cluster is the largest in India, manufacturing leather shoes, sports shoes, sandals, chappals, and juttis for domestic retail chains, e-commerce marketplaces, and export buyers across the Middle East, Europe, and Africa. Thousands of small and large units operate side by side, supplying similar product ranges through overlapping wholesale and online distribution channels.

With counterfeit and look-alike footwear frequently sold under copied brand names and imitation packaging through unauthorised retailers and online marketplaces, brand protection through trademark registration is critical. Fake footwear damages customer trust and undercuts genuine manufacturers on price and reputation. A registered trademark gives you the exclusive legal right to your brand name and logo across India, enabling action against imitators through cease-and-desist notices, e-commerce takedowns, customs recordal, and court injunctions.
