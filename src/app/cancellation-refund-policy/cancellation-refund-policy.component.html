At Trademarx (“we,” “us,” or “our”), we provide professional intellectual property and business registration services, including trademark registration, trademark renewal, MSME (Udyam) registration, ISO certification assistance, IEC registration, and related filings. This Cancellation and Refund Policy explains the terms under which cancellations and refunds are handled for orders placed through trademarx.in.

1. Cancellation Before Filing

You may request cancellation of your order at no cost within 24 hours of payment, provided that we have not yet commenced any filing, drafting, or submission with the relevant government authority (such as the Trademark Registry, Ministry of MSME, or ISO certifying body) on your behalf.

2. Cancellation After Filing Has Commenced

Once our team has commenced work on your application — including drafting documents, preparing filings, or submitting your application to the relevant government portal — the professional service fee becomes non-refundable, as the effort and resources committed cannot be reversed. Any statutory or government fee already paid to a government authority on your behalf is non-refundable under any circumstances, since such fees are collected and retained by the respective government department and not by Trademarx.

3. Refund Eligibility

Refunds of the professional service fee may be considered in the following situations:

  • Cancellation requested within 24 hours of payment and before any filing work has begun.
  • Duplicate payment made for the same order due to a technical or payment gateway error.
  • Trademarx is unable to provide the requested service due to reasons attributable solely to us.

Refunds will not be provided where the application is rejected, objected to, or opposed by the relevant government authority for reasons outside our control, or where incorrect or incomplete information was provided by the applicant.

5. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed to the original mode of payment within 7–10 business days, subject to processing timelines of the respective bank or payment gateway.
