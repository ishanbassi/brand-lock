Why It Matters

Why You Should Track Your Application's Status

Catch Objections in Time

Miss the 30-day window to reply to an objection and your application can be treated as abandoned — often before you even receive the physical notice.

Never Miss a Renewal

A registered mark is only protected for 10 years. Track the date so renewal fees never sneak past and hand your brand to a lapse.

Confirm Before You Grow

Signing a franchise deal, listing on a marketplace, or raising funds? Lenders and partners will ask for proof of status — have it ready.

Spot Opposition Early

A competitor opposing your mark has a fixed 2-month reply window. Checking status regularly is the only way to know it's happened.
