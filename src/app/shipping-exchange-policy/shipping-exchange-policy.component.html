Trademarx (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides digital intellectual property and business registration services, including trademark registration, trademark renewal, MSME (Udyam) registration, ISO certification assistance, IEC registration, and related filings. As these are professional services delivered electronically, we do not ship any physical products, and this policy explains how service “delivery” and exchanges work instead.

1. Nature of Delivery

All services offered on trademarx.in are delivered digitally. There is no physical shipment involved. Upon successful payment, our team begins processing your application, and all deliverables — including filing acknowledgements, government receipts, certificates, and correspondence — are shared with you electronically via email and/or your account dashboard.

2. Turnaround Time

Typical turnaround times for filing and acknowledgement are shared with you at the time of order confirmation, and generally range from 24 hours to a few business days, depending on the service selected. Timelines for final approval, registration, or certification are determined by the relevant government authority and are outside our control.

3. Exchange of Services

As our services are customized to the information you provide (such as your brand name, business category, or applicant details), they cannot be “exchanged” in the way a physical product would be. If you notice an error in the details submitted for your application, please contact us as soon as possible so we can correct it before filing is submitted to the relevant authority. Once a filing has been submitted, changes may require additional government fees and procedures as prescribed by the concerned authority.

4. Non-Delivery or Delays

If you do not receive an acknowledgement or update within the communicated turnaround time, please reach out to our support team so we can investigate and resolve the issue promptly.
