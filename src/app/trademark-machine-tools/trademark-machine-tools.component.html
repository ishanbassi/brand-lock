Why Machine Tools Manufacturers in Punjab Need Trademark Protection

Ludhiana and the surrounding industrial belt — Focal Point, Industrial Area A & B, Sherpur Kalan, and Sahnewal — are home to hundreds of machine tools units manufacturing lathes, vertical milling machines, radial drilling machines, surface grinders, power presses, and CNC machining centres.
