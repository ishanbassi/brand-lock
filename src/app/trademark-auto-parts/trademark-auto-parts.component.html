Why Auto Parts Manufacturers in Punjab Need Trademark Protection

Industrial clusters like Focal Point, Industrial Area A & B, Gill Road, and Tajpur Road in Ludhiana host thousands of manufacturers of auto parts, spare parts, bearings, fasteners, forgings and machine components. Brand names and packaging designs on these products are frequently copied — counterfeit parts sold under look-alike branding damage your reputation, create safety liability, and divert revenue to copycats.

A registered trademark gives you the legal right to act against counterfeiters, builds credibility with OEM buyers and export customers, and is often required before you can register on B2B marketplaces like IndiaMART, TradeIndia, or Amazon Business.
