Why Brassware & Brass Handicrafts Manufacturers in Moradabad Need Trademark Protection

Moradabad is home to one of the world's largest brass manufacturing clusters, producing brass statues, decorative ornaments, tableware, lamps, and artware that are supplied to domestic markets and exported to the US, Europe, and the Middle East through export houses and B2B platforms.

With thousands of units producing similar decorative and utility items through overlapping export networks, copied brand names embossed on brass pieces, duplicated catalogues, and imitation packaging reaching the same international buyers are well-known hazards in this industry. A registered trademark gives you the exclusive legal right to your brand name and logo across India — enabling you to record your mark with Indian Customs, and act against counterfeiters through cease-and-desist notices, civil court injunctions, and takedowns on e-commerce and B2B export platforms.
