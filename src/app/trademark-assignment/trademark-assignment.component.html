What Is Trademark Assignment?

  • A trademark can be transferred (assigned) to another person or company
  • Assignment can be with or without the goodwill of the business
  • Recorded via Form TM-P at the Trademark Registry
  • Without proper recording, the assignee cannot enforce rights
  • We draft the assignment deed and file TM-P on your behalf
