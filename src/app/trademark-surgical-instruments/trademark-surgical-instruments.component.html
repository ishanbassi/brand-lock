Why Surgical Instrument Manufacturers in Punjab Need Trademark Protection

Jalandhar's surgical instruments cluster is one of the oldest and largest of its kind in the world, manufacturing surgical scissors, forceps, scalpels, dental instruments, veterinary instruments, and beauty grooming tools that are exported to CE and FDA-regulated markets across Europe, the US, the Middle East, and beyond.

With hundreds of units producing similar-looking instruments through overlapping export agents, copied brand names on packaging, look-alike catalogues, and grey-market counterfeits reaching the same overseas buyers are well-known hazards in this industry. A registered trademark gives you the exclusive legal right to your brand name and logo across India — enabling you to record your mark with Indian Customs, and act against counterfeiters through cease-and-desist notices, civil court injunctions, and takedowns on B2B export platforms.
