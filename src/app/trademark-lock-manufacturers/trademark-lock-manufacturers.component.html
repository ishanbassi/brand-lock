Why Lock & Padlock Manufacturers in Aligarh Need Trademark Protection

Aligarh is home to one of India's largest lock manufacturing clusters, producing padlocks, door locks, mortise locks, cabinet locks, and hardware fittings that are supplied to hardware dealers across India and exported to markets worldwide.

With thousands of units producing near-identical products through overlapping dealer networks, copied brand names embossed on lock bodies, duplicated packaging, and imitation keys reaching the same hardware markets are well-known hazards in this industry. A registered trademark gives you the exclusive legal right to your brand name and logo across India — enabling you to record your mark with Indian Customs, and act against counterfeiters through cease-and-desist notices, civil court injunctions, and takedowns on e-commerce and B2B export platforms.
