THE STATUTORY & ECONOMIC DANGER

Why Waiting for Official Journal Publication is 12 Months Too Late

Traditional docketing software only triggers when a mark appears in the Trade Marks Journal (TMJ). For Indian IP attorneys and brands, this delay causes irreversible commercial damage.

hourglass_disabled
Pitfall 01

The 4-to-12 Month Gazette Lag

Official Trade Marks Journal (TMJ) publication takes months or even years post-filing. By the time the gazette issues, the infringing party has already printed packaging, launched digital ad campaigns, and generated consumer goodwill.

Result: Costly market entrenchment and stubborn counter-arguments of 'honest concurrent use'.
search_off
Pitfall 02

The Ex-Parte Examination Blackbox

Trademark Examiners regularly miss deceptive phonetic similarities when drafting the First Examination Report (FER) under Section 11. Early detection allows you to place an authoritative Pre-Grant Representation directly before the Examiner.

Result: Refusal at the threshold without entering into lengthy Form TM-O opposition litigation.
payments
Pitfall 03

Litigation Costs Multiply 10x

Serving a Cease & Desist within 48 hours of filing stops the applicant before they invest capital. A single C&D or pre-grant caution letter costs negligible attorney time, whereas High Court commercial suits run into tens of lakhs.

Result: 80%+ voluntary abandonment or amicable mark withdrawal at Form TM-M stage.
