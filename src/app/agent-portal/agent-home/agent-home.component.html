Frequently Asked Questions

Simple, honest answers for attorneys evaluating Trademarx.

Do I need my CGPDTM portal login password? expand_more

No. All trademark records, journal publications, and cause lists are matters of public legal record under The Trade Marks Act, 1999. Trademarx operates by indexing these statutory records directly. We will never ask for your CGPDTM digital signature passwords or confidential chamber credentials.

Is the Free Tier really free forever, or is it a trial? expand_more

It is genuinely free forever for up to 50 active trademarks. Junior advocates and independent attorneys do not need to provide a credit card to get started and manage their initial client docket.

How does the WhatsApp alert integration work? expand_more

Alerts are delivered via official Meta verified business channels. Messages strictly report public registry deadlines, weekly gazette opposition clashes, and hearing board listings, formatted with one-tap client sharing buttons.
