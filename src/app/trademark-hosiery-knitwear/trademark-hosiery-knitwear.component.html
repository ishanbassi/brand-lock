Why Hosiery & Knitwear Manufacturers in Punjab Need Trademark Protection

Ludhiana's Focal Point, Sherpur Kalan, and Haibowal industrial clusters are home to thousands of units producing woollen sweaters, cardigans, shawls, socks, thermal wear, cotton knitwear, and sportswear for domestic retail and international export. As Ludhiana brands build recognition with buyers in the US, Europe, and the Middle East, imitators copy garment packaging, labels, and brand names to ride on that reputation.

A registered trademark gives you the exclusive legal right to your brand name and logo across India. It is a mandatory requirement for listing products on Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra, and is increasingly demanded by export buyers, institutional supply contracts, and retail chains. With a registered trademark you can pursue infringers through cease-and-desist notices, customs recordal, and court injunctions.
