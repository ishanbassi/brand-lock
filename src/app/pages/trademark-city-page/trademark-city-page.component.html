1

Free Trademark Search

We search 15M+ existing trademarks to check if your brand name is available. You get a detailed search report before filing.

Application Filing

Our agents file your application with IP India under the correct trademark class. Your mark gets protection from this filing date onward.

3

Examination

IP India examines your application (typically within 1–3 months). We handle any examination reports or objections on your behalf.

4

Publication & Registration

Your mark is published in the Trade Marks Journal for public opposition. After 4 months with no opposition, the certificate is issued.
