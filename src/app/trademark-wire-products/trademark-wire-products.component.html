Why Wire Drawing & Wire Mesh Manufacturers in Punjab Need Trademark Protection

Ludhiana and its surrounding industrial belt form one of India's largest wire products clusters, manufacturing binding wire, GI wire, steel wire, wire mesh, wire netting, and wire nails that are supplied to hardware dealers, construction sites, and agricultural users across India and exported abroad.

With hundreds of ancillary units producing near-identical coils through overlapping dealer networks, copied brand names stamped on wire spools, duplicated bundle tags, and imitation packaging reaching the same hardware markets are well-known hazards in this industry. A registered trademark gives you the exclusive legal right to your brand name and logo across India — enabling you to record your mark with Indian Customs, and act against counterfeiters through cease-and-desist notices, civil court injunctions, and takedowns on B2B export platforms.
