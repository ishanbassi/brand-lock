Why Forging Units in Punjab Need Trademark Protection

Ludhiana's industrial belt hosts a dense cluster of forging units — drop-forging shops, closed-die forging manufacturers, and precision forging units that supply crankshafts, connecting rods, flanges, gear blanks, and forged rings to OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers, and the replacement parts market across India and globally. These units power supply chains for the automotive, tractor, bicycle, railway, and construction sectors.

With hundreds of forging units competing on similar grades and tolerances, brand identity is a key differentiator for commanding premium pricing and winning supply contracts. Counterfeit stamping on forged parts — especially in the automotive replacement market — is a serious and recurring problem. A registered trademark gives you the exclusive legal right to your brand name and logo across India, enabling you to take action against imitators through cease-and-desist notices, customs recordal, and court injunctions.
