Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how to download a trademark certificate online in India.

search

How do I find my application number?

Use the number on your Form TM-A acknowledgement. You can also search by trademark name on this page to find the matching application record.

hourglass_empty

Why is my certificate not available?

Trademark registration certificate download is available only when IP India records the mark as Registered.

Pending, objected, opposed, refused and abandoned marks do not have a registration certificate.
