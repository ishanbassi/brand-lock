What Is a Trademark Objection?

  • After filing, the Examiner issues a report citing objections to your mark
  • You have 30 days to file a written reply countering the objections
  • Failing to reply leads to automatic abandonment of your application
  • A strong reply can clear objections without a hearing
  • Common objections: similarity, descriptiveness, non-distinctiveness
